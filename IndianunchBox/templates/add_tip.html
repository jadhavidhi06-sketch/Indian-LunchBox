Title: Grandma's Secret to Fluffy Idlis

Tip: After grinding the batter, let it ferment overnight in a warm place. Add a pinch of fenugreek seeds while grinding - this makes the idlis extra soft and gives them a beautiful aroma. Never stir the batter once fermented; gently scoop it into the idli molds!

Why it works: Fenugreek seeds act as a natural fermenting agent and the overnight fermentation develops the perfect texture.
